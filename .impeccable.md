## Design Context

### Users
The homepage serves a mixed audience: students and technology professionals looking for career growth, and companies, entrepreneurs, and public-sector leaders looking for business connections and innovation opportunities. Users need to quickly understand what CG Tech Day is, which part of the event is for them, why attending is valuable, and how to register.

### Brand Personality
Regional, ambitious, connected. The experience should feel like Campo Grande/MS is confidently presenting its technology ecosystem: accessible enough for first-time attendees, credible enough for executives, and distinctive enough to feel rooted in place rather than like a generic tech conference.

### Aesthetic Direction
Pantanal Futurista: a fusion of technology, civic innovation, and the natural identity of Mato Grosso do Sul. The visual language should combine digital grids, flowing river-like paths, soft topographic layers, deep blues, greens, and controlled yellow accents. Avoid generic SaaS gradients, endless identical cards, and cold corporate tech visuals that could belong to any city.

### Design Principles
- Make the event feel local and future-facing at the same time.
- Balance career and business audiences without making either feel secondary.
- Use flow-based layouts, diagonals, paths, and asymmetric compositions to avoid repetitive card grids.
- Keep CTAs direct and visible, but avoid making every section feel equally loud.
- Use motion and visual effects sparingly, prioritizing clarity, performance, and accessibility.
